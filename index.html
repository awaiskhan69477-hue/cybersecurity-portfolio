04 / INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW

How I approach a security alert.

A portfolio view of the reasoning process practiced across my labs—not a claim of production SOC experience.

01

Validate

Understand the alert, affected host/user, timestamp and available telemetry.

02

Correlate

Search related authentication, process, endpoint or network events in the available logs.

03

Investigate

04

Document

Record evidence, observations, impact and recommended next steps clearly.
